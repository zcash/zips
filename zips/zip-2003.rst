Abstract
========

This proposal disallows all v4 transactions from NU7 onward. It therefore affects
every workflow that creates v4 transactions, not only workflows involving Sprout.
Neither the v5 transaction format [#zip-0225]_ introduced in the NU5 network
upgrade, nor the v6 format [#zip-0229]_ introduced in NU6.3, supports Sprout.
Because Sprout transfers can only be represented in v4 transactions, this proposal
also disables the ability to spend Sprout funds.

It is not proposed in this ZIP to unissue, burn, or otherwise make Sprout funds
permanently unavailable. This leaves open the theoretical possibility of re-enabling
v4 transactions, or of adding another facility to retrieve these funds if the Zcash
community considers it worthwhile. However, the ability to spend Sprout funds may
never be re-enabled.


Motivation
==========

Zcash is an extremely complex protocol. Some of that complexity comes from
functionality that is now obsolete.

The Sprout shielded protocol was the first large-scale deployment of
general-purpose zero-knowledge proofs, but suffered from long proving times
and high memory requirements that limited its practicality. Its successors,
the Sapling and Orchard shielded protocols, made substantial optimizations
[#cultivating-sapling]_ and added new functionality such as viewing keys and
diversified addresses. As a result, Sprout is essentially unused at this point.

In the Sapling network upgrade, the proving system and the pairing-friendly
curves used for Sprout were changed to be the same as for the Sapling shielded
protocol, i.e. Groth16 and BLS12-381. So that part of the attack surface is
shared between Sprout and Sapling. The magnitude of potential balance violation
due to any weakness in Sprout’s cryptography is also limited by the “turnstile”
mechanism defined in ZIP 209 [#zip-0209]_.

Nevertheless, the parts of Sprout that are not shared with Sapling, such as the
JoinSplit circuit and the handling of Sprout nullifiers and note commitments,
still impose a substantial burden on live transaction-acceptance paths before NU7.
For instance, verification of transactions involving Sprout JoinSplit descriptions
has to be taken into account for denial-of-service attacks against mempool and new-
block processing. Because of Sprout’s lack of use, there is little incentive for
node implementations to optimize its verification, which may lead to such attacks
imposing greater cost than they otherwise would.

The deprecation of zcashd, just prior to the NU6.3 upgrade, also removed the
only maintained software that still provided wallet functionality for Sprout.
Although the consensus rules still permit moving Sprout funds before NU7, this was
and is another reason to give holders sufficient warning of difficulty in retrieving
them.


Privacy Implications
====================

Moving value out of the Sprout pool changes its publicly visible chain value pool
balance through the ``vpub_old`` and ``vpub_new`` fields [#zip-0209]_. The amount
moved by a transaction is therefore visible. A short or poorly coordinated exit
period could also make transaction timing and distinctive amounts easier to
correlate with particular holders.

Disallowing v4 transactions does not itself disclose additional information about
notes left in Sprout, but it prevents those notes from being spent unless a future
consensus change restores a mechanism for doing so.


Requirements
============

It must become possible to remove the complexity and attack surface of Sprout
from Zcash specifications and node implementations.


Specification
=============

In § 7.1.2 ‘Transaction Consensus Rules’ [#protocol-txnconsensus]_ (as of NU6.3),
change:

  * [NU6.3 onward] The transaction version number MUST be 4 or 5 or 6.

to

  * [NU6.3 only, pre-NU7] The transaction version number MUST be 4 or 5 or 6.
  * [NU7 onward] The transaction version number MUST be 5 or 6.

These changes apply identically to Mainnet and Testnet.

Interaction with the proposed Network Sustainability Mechanism
--------------------------------------------------------------

For clarity, the Sprout chain value pool balance as of activation of this ZIP
remains issued, and is therefore included in $\mathsf{IssuedSupply}$ (as defined in
§ 4.17 ‘Chain Value Pool Balances’ [#protocol-chainvaluepoolbalances]_) for the
purpose of calculations in Network Sustainability Mechanism ZIPs that affect
issuance, including ZIP 237 [#zip-0237]_.


Deployment
==========

This ZIP is proposed to be deployed with NU7 [#zip-0259]_.
